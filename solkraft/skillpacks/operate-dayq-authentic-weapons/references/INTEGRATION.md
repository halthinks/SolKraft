Combat/damage/wounds, armor, acoustic propagation, movement/traversal, exos/mechs/vehicles, AI behavior, construction, authority, persistence, economy, and assets retain their state. The separate library-evaluation task alone may recommend a backend; no vendor type may cross the boundary without an approved adapter/migration.
