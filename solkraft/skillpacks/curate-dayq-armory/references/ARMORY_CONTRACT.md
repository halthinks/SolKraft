# DayQ Armory Contract

Every weapon family records a stable ID, reference identity, intended shipping identity, category, canon source, rarity, operating family, feed family, ammunition compatibility, physical envelope, mass/balance targets, control states, moving parts, sockets, optic/mount keys, magazine keys, muzzle keys, sling/hand/support keys, collision/obstruction geometry, condition and repair states, root materials, acquisition/repair/salvage routes, progression, asset route, Unreal definition, authority/persistence owners, and evidence state.

The initial catalog is a production backlog, not evidence that 60 finished models exist. Real-world references provide dimensional and operational baselines. The DayQ factory must create lawful production masters and canonical wear/repair variants. Public pictures are not automatically reusable assets.

Compatibility is explicit and many-to-many. An optic fits only when footprint or mount adapter, rail/ring standard, available length, recoil/load rating, clearance, height, eye relief, and weapon state all pass. Attachments alter physical mass, balance, snag envelope, ready time, sight height, noise/flash, reliability, repair, storage footprint, and persistence.

The same firearm instance retains exact receiver/core identity, serialized or filed-off state, barrel and action components, furniture, sights, attachments, magazine, chamber, selector/safety, condition, fouling, lubrication, temperature, contamination, repair history, ownership, provenance, zero, and revision. Inventory and persistence remain authoritative.

Representative acceptance must cover at least one closed-bolt magazine-fed carbine, open-bolt SMG, delayed/blowback SMG, belt-fed weapon, manual precision rifle, service pistol, pump shotgun, single-shot launcher, revolver launcher, and mounted automatic launcher. Shared animation or component standards do not erase family-specific handling.
