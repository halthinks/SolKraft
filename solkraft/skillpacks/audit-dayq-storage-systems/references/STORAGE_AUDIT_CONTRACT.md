# Storage Audit Contract

Audit:

1. catalog and component completeness;
2. dimensions, volume, opening and shape fit;
3. typed slots and component compatibility;
4. mass, center of load, support and structural ratings;
5. closure, retention, security, environment and hazard behavior;
6. nesting, stacking and anti-duplication;
7. repair, salvage, recipes and progression;
8. asset-family/factory/Blender/Unreal lineage;
9. authority, contention, persistence and migration;
10. placement, stocking, depletion and economy;
11. UI/controller access and interaction timing;
12. network, save, actor, physics, material and density budgets.

File existence, a generated prompt, mesh import or compilation is not storage acceptance.
