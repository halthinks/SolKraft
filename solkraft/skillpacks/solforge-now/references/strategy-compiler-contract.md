# Sol Strategy Compiler contract

The compiler derives its ten public skills, internal execution modules, workflow reachability, live effect-adapter health, and Advanced availability from one hash-bound capability registry. AUTO recommends only the public surface. Advanced exposes every reachable option with the same ranking evidence and marks unavailable adapters without allowing acceptance.

Preference profiles may influence ranking dimensions and explicit non-authority choices. Privacy-minimized outcome telemetry may influence scores only after the minimum evidence threshold. Neither mechanism may affect authority, consequential classification, approval, effect scope, prohibitions, or permission expansion. Goals are informational summaries only and are excluded from execution authority and control.

Accepted strategies create a common hash-bound artifact handoff envelope containing the objective, result, claims and evidence, artifacts, mutations and effects, authority consumed, uncertainty, acceptance, next capabilities, and recovery. Candidate policy changes remain shadow-only until offline champion/challenger gates pass and a versioned promotion receipt is released with rollback.

The compiler models `intent → evidence method → completion effort → skill → workflow → topology → rigor → execution strategy → effect plan → authority → acceptance → landing` as a typed dependency graph. An optional informational Goal summary is derived after approval and is not a graph gate.

- Apply hard capability and authority constraints before deterministic multi-objective scoring.
- Explicit consequential verbs and contextually necessary terminal landing effects are classified before incidental topic/evidence keywords. Direct effects bind their exact action-family workflow; compound work binds a normal implementation workflow plus an ordered effect plan. Every external step requires a live adapter, Level 3 authority, the `exact_consequential_effect` boundary, and receipt landing or compilation fails closed. Goal presence and Goal status are irrelevant. Pure answers select the non-mutating answer workflow and chat-only effect plan.
- Score outcome quality, completeness, latency, cost, risk control, reversibility, evidence strength, and user preference under a hash-bound versioned policy.
- Completion effort is exactly Low, Medium, High, or Extra under the versioned effort catalog. It compiles a concrete live route containing primary and supporting workflows, execution skills, tools, topology, rigor, evidence and verification depth, critic depth, iteration limit, gap-closure behavior, relative effort, and waste policy.
- Low selects one lightest-sufficient primary route. Medium may add one material helper. High may add two independent specialist or verification lanes. Extra may add up to four relevant live routes, use Ultra only when natively available and justified, and performs maximum bounded closure. No level may expand objective, authority, effects, permissions, or approval.
- Show exactly the top three by default. Every option includes the same score vector, rationale, counterfactual downstream change, unlocked capability, added risk, confidence, signals, selection source, and what would change the ranking.
- Advanced is reachable at every determination and contains every live valid option. A displayed option must be callable; a callable workflow must be reachable.
- Ask only a question whose answer can materially alter the route or authority contract.
- Run the independent bounded critic before final review. It may keep the winner or promote a stronger option, with its findings recorded.
- Every determination requires an explicit server transition, including an unchanged AUTO winner. UI `Next` and user-authored chat `Next` share the cursor and confirmation ledger; explicit chat `EXECUTE` may deterministically confirm the remaining displayed AUTO winners and accept the resulting final contract. Browser-local navigation and model-inferred approval cannot reach final review. Every pending Next or Execute waits indefinitely without expiry, polling, background turns, or token use. On mount or reconnect, the app must restore the durable cursor before rendering any prior snapshot; after an uncertain transition response it performs one read-only restore and never repeats the mutation. The current unpressed Next or Execute control pulses.
- Steering changes the selected node and its descendants only, preserves independent selections, emits a before/after diff, truncates stale descendant confirmations, and revokes any stale acceptance. Future crumbs are disabled.
- The effect-plan determination ranks verified local completion, configured remote synchronization, live production synchronization, and exact external actions as applicable. It shows every ordered step, authority source, provider, target, adapter health, receipt requirement, and downstream authority consequence. A required but unavailable landing remains selected and visibly blocked; it never silently degrades to local-only completion.
- Final Accept or Deny is unavailable until the ordered confirmation ledger covers every determination. Pending and denied strategies fail closed against prompt generation and continuation bypasses only inside their exact typed task, request, program, or decision lineage. Global recency is never a selector: an unrelated pending, deferred, denied, or archived decision cannot block or redirect another program, and a contextual identifier cannot impersonate an explicit resume target. Multiple genuinely connected matches render a human-readable chooser with task name, decision title, summary, and creation time.
- Final acceptance hash-binds the graph, catalog, policy, effort catalog, concrete completion route, ordered effect plan, adapters, selections, exact authority and effects, evidence, constraints, stop conditions, recovery, acceptance, and landing. Acceptance is the execution-authority boundary and starts the approved plan immediately. It is not followed by duplicate approval for unchanged effects. The exact instruction is: `Say EXECUTE and I'll execute the approved plan. I'll also create an informational Goal only to keep the work on track.` Goal creation is optional and non-blocking.
- The compiler may build any finite custom execution sequence from registry-backed phase contracts. Every phase declares a stable identifier, dependency edges, execution adapter, inputs, outputs, evidence verifier, retry/recovery policy, stop conditions, and done-when contract. Planning continuations and setup widgets are never execution adapters. The compiler rejects duplicate IDs, missing dependencies, cycles, and unavailable adapters before final acceptance.
- A custom sequence is one immutable Decision Program. Its final Scope view displays the entire topologically ordered program. One `solforge-program-acceptance-receipt` covers every displayed phase and Execute immediately starts the first dependency-ready phase. `solforge_continue_decision_program` returns each next exact adapter, and the accepted domain receipt advances automatically to the next phase. No unchanged phase gets another approval; only a material Scope, authority, permission, target, or external-effect revision returns to an approval gate. Pause, Stop, and Resume apply directly to and preserve the same program and receipt lineage. Any Goal is a verbose plan-completion pointer only and cannot authorize, block, pause, resume, stop, route, recover, lease, lock, or complete execution.
- Feedback records prediction-versus-outcome evidence against the receipt and policy. `solforge_propose_strategy_policy_update` may turn that evidence into a transparent candidate, but never activates it. A policy change requires a new version, code review, promotion evidence, tests, official release, and rollback target.
