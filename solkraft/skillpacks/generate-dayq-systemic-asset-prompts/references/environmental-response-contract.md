# Environmental Response Contract

Every asset classifies wetting, saturation, drainage, drying, freezing/thawing, corrosion, rot, swelling, warping, electrical ingress, chemical/radiological contamination, mud/dust, heat/fire, and immersion as applicable, not applicable with justification, or unknown-needs-evidence.

Sample roof/cover fraction, enclosure and seal state, damaged openings, wind-driven rain, splash, pooling, ground contact, immersion, temperature, humidity, sun, wind, and elapsed time. Partition meaningful material regions and persist their wetness, absorbed/pooled water, temperature, degradation, contamination, seal damage, and last-simulated time.

Connect applicable responses to mass, center of mass, friction, strength, insulation, conductivity, noise, visibility, operation, repair, and salvage. Each response needs inputs, rule/curve, thresholds, failure/recovery, persistence, and validation. Powered assets address ingress/isolation/short/fire/dry-out/safe restart. Absorbent assets address uptake/added mass/drying/rot/swelling/warping. Sealed assets require rated and damaged-state evidence.
