# DayQ systemic asset contract

## Required checklist

- Real dimensions, mass, construction, materials, capacity, operating ratings, and hazards.
- Pre-collapse purpose and a plausible causal history across day 15, day 30, the three-year assault, and year 28.
- Present owner, modifications, condition, survival function, scarcity, faction value, and trade value.
- Recipe, prerequisite knowledge/research, stations, tools, energy, labor, specialists, failure, and salvage.
- Relevant electrical, mechanical, fuel, water, chemical, medical, ballistic, structural, communications, radiation, contamination, or environmental behavior.
- Named components, sockets, pivots, interaction/inventory points, zones, blockers, connections, emitters, and states.
- LODs, collision, physics, materials, persistence, replication, metadata, Blender production, engine integration, renders, and tests.

## Diesel generator quality baseline

Create a repairable diesel generator for DayQ using realistic dimensions, mass, welded frame construction, engine displacement, fuel capacity, alternator output, starter battery, cooling system, exhaust, electrical controls, grounding, and plausible load-dependent fuel consumption. Make condition affect output stability, noise radius, heat signature, exhaust visibility, efficiency, starting reliability, and failure risk. Separate the battery, starter, alternator, radiator, fuel tank, filters, belts, control panel, outlets, grounding point, access panels, frame, engine mounts, and exhaust into inspectable or replaceable components. Support refueling, contaminated fuel, overload, short circuits, overheating, sabotage, theft, transport, improper grounding, rain exposure, corrosion, repair, and base-grid integration. Require a clean hero mesh, mobile-aware LOD chain, compound collision, moving/removable parts, power and interaction sockets, dry, wet, muddy, burned, repaired, contaminated, cartel-modified, clan-modified, powered, overloaded, and faulted states. Export gameplay, electrical, physics, damage, repair, salvage, persistence, audio, heat, and exhaust metadata. Validate with turntable, collision, component, powered, load-change, fault, damage, nighttime, replication, persistence, and engine-integration evidence.

## Category routing

- Medicine/protection: dose, sterility, fit, material barrier, storage, expiration, misuse, contamination, and body interaction.
- Weapons/ballistics: caliber/energy, action, feeding, wear, heat, recoil, armor/material response, safety, repair, and authoritative firing state.
- Power/electrical: voltage, current, output curve, protection, grounding, heat, fuel/energy, load, faults, and connections.
- Water/agriculture: flow, pressure, quality, filtration, irrigation, contamination, seasonal output, wear, and biological consequences.
- Vehicles/drones: drivetrain or propulsion, control, payload, energy/fuel, sensors, occupants, damage zones, latency, and recovery.
- Robotic/post-quantum: physical root of trust, sensors, relays, power, communications, shielding, fallback modes, and target-specific interaction.
- Buildings/base pieces: structure, joints, support, weather, fire, breach, navigation, utilities, placement, permissions, and decay.
