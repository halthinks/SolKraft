# Glentech fabrication contract

## Independent ladders

- R0-R5 measures reclamation and material qualification.
- F0-F5 measures furnace/foundry stations: Camp Hearth, Wheel Forge, Crucible Furnace, Electric/Induction Furnace, Controlled Alloy Foundry, Heavy Foundry.
- P0-P7 measures one physical machine: Ruined/Partial, Home Polymer, Reinforced Fabricator, Circuit Cell, Metaljet Foundry, Multi-Material Works, Heavy Systems Foundry, Sovereign Integration Cell.
- G0-G7 measures complete clan/base manufacturing capability.
- M0-M7 measures an item's manufacturability, from field work to sovereign hardware.

An item may require all four relevant gates plus character knowledge and physical components.

## Concise R1 forge

Powered route: one suitable wheel/brake-drum/grill/fire-bowl/stove body, one hair-dryer/vehicle-HVAC/shop blower or hand-crank fan, one metal air pipe, common forge material, ingot mold, and handling tool. No-power route replaces powered air with bellows made from leather/heavy cloth, sticks/boards, rope/bindings, and the same pipe.

Track fuel or manual/powered operation, operating time, supported material tier, output quality, visible smoke/light/noise, overall condition, and repair. Do not require management of grates, lining panels, controls, or internal furnace subassemblies.

## Physical machine progression

Advance by either installing visible frame/chamber/cooling/toolhead/controller/power/filtration/safety/calibration upgrades into the existing machine or constructing/recovering a visibly newer model. Both routes require material, seed capability, power, tools, transport, downtime, calibration, qualification, provenance, condition, and repair.

## Thermal-Gradient Jet Deposition

This fictional P4+ process uses a compatible graded cassette, sealed melt/filter/metering, replaceable jet/nozzle, actively supercooled build surface, major power and cooling, shielding/interlocks, filtration/waste capture, sensors, calibration, post-processing, and inspection. Mixed metal cannot become precision material. Do not provide actionable real-world construction parameters.

## Bounded reproduction

A capable parent may fabricate lower-tier structural parts, boards, wiring guides, toolhead parts, and low-performance controller logic only with feedstock, power, external processes, wear, calibration artifacts, seed parts, assembly, independent qualification, and a signed child identity. It cannot freely reproduce precision bearings, optics, power semiconductors, certified PQC roots, advanced compute, or Quantum ASICs.

## Locked energy-industry integration

The user locked a manufactured-storage Stirling spiral. A damaged P0/P1 Glentech machine becomes useful after an empty player-made B0 battery receives an earned first charge. It prints only qualified supporting parts within its capability; the concise F1 forge makes rough stock; recovered precision donors supply what neither can produce.

LS0-LS4 require visibly increasing P capability plus matching R/F/G/M, metrology, material, power, cooling, installation and agent-design capability. No printer alone creates a complete generator, battery chemistry, converter, refinery, foundry, vessel or hydrogen system.

Agents inspect actual clan inventory and propose bounded substitutions whose efficiency, lifespan, noise, stability, maintenance or failure costs remain visible. Do not reduce the machine to a fixed token list, certificate hunt, or automatic blueprint.

Compact plastic conversion produces crude burner oil for external heat and Stirling use. A substantial physical upgrade or workshop-scale replacement produces engine-compatible synthetic gasoline for repaired municipal peak generators. Old residue and crude plastic oil remain rejected by gasoline consumers.
