# Direct DayQ Player Factions

Treat origin as permanent cultural history, not biology or current clan membership. Load [the faction contract](references/FACTION_CONTRACT.md) before changing faction content or data.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the current origin catalog, progression catalog, decision record, reputation owner, death policy, and persistence schema.
2. Preserve exactly four canon origins: The Warforge Clans, The Ash Hounds, The Bastion Directorate, and The Ghostwire Cells.
3. Keep origin, current affiliation, reputation, clan membership, and learned competency as separate fields owned by their existing systems.
4. Map every origin affordance to a real gameplay-system interface. Never add unexplained passive damage, free resources, wall vision, instant healing, or one-button hacking.
5. Apply the canonical tier rule: chosen origin starts at T1 in its four native trees and T0 elsewhere; T1-T5 remain cross-trainable; only matching-origin T6 exists in that character's authorized projection.
6. Strip foreign T6 records before replication, UI projection, save export, analytics payloads, logs visible to clients, and migration results.
7. Validate schemas and run Unreal faction/progression automation before claiming implementation evidence.

## Ownership boundaries

- This skill owns origin canon, origin-to-tree mapping, character-creation presentation, inherited cultural history, burdens, starting-package classes, and native T6 eligibility.
- Reputation owns relationship transactions. Clans own membership. Progression owns evidence and unlock state. Inventory owns item grants. Persistence owns journals and migrations.
- Do not create parallel authoritative state.

## Required proof

Require all four origin initializations, exact four-tree T1 starts, twelve-tree T0 starts, clan/defection invariance, no foreign T6 payload fields, forged-request rejection, restart/migration survival, and a no-core-objective-requires-T6 audit.
