# img2threejs Production Contract

Use img2threejs for reviewable hard-surface and modular prototypes: props, tools, improvised construction, furniture, generators, barricades, turrets, drones, bunker machinery, post-quantum devices, interiors, and concept vehicles. Do not make it final authority for realistic humans, high-fidelity organic creatures, complex cloth, exact hidden mechanisms/interiors, cinematic deformation, or final skeletal assets.

Required sequence:

1. reference suitability and hidden-geometry assessment;
2. identity-defining detail inventory and scale cues;
3. deep ObjectSculptSpec with hierarchy, materials, pivots, sockets, colliders, destruction groups, and environmental material regions;
4. strict-quality validation;
5. locked passes: `blockout`, `structural-pass`, `form-refinement`, `material-pass`, `surface-pass`, `lighting-pass`, `interaction-pass`, `optimization-pass`;
6. real fixed-view render and comparison sheet for every pass;
7. overall and feature-level scores against declared thresholds;
8. exactly one review decision: `continue`, `refine-spec`, `refine-code`, `request-input`, or `stop`;
9. accepted prototype and ObjectSculptSpec become a Blender production contract, never a substitute for it.

Do not advance a pass without evidence. Use `refine-spec` when interpretation is wrong and `refine-code` when implementation fails an adequate specification.
