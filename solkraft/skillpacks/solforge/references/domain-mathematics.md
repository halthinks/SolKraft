# Mathematics workflows

Select the capability matching the actual task. Read its entrypoint only when needed.

- [solforge-workflow-math-compute](../../solforge-workflow-math-compute/SKILL.md): Design reproducible symbolic or numerical experiments that inform—but do not substitute for—proof.
- [solforge-workflow-math-counterexample](../../solforge-workflow-math-counterexample/SKILL.md): Stress the statement across boundary cases, constructions, computation, and known obstruction families.
- [solforge-workflow-math-explain](../../solforge-workflow-math-explain/SKILL.md): Produce a level-appropriate explanation with intuition, formal steps, examples, diagrams, and limitations.
- [solforge-workflow-math-literature](../../solforge-workflow-math-literature/SKILL.md): Map related results, equivalent formulations, known techniques, and unresolved gaps with source provenance.
- [solforge-workflow-math-prove](../../solforge-workflow-math-prove/SKILL.md): Construct and audit a proof using independent approaches, lemma tracking, and adversarial counterexample checks.
- [solforge-workflow-math-solve](../../solforge-workflow-math-solve/SKILL.md): Develop a rigorous solution or derivation with checked assumptions, intermediate results, and explicit limits.
- [solforge-workflow-math-verify](../../solforge-workflow-math-verify/SKILL.md): Check every inference, dependency, edge case, and hidden assumption; isolate gaps and propose bounded repairs.
