# Research -> Mastery -> Perfection Super-Loop Directive

Use the original accepted objective **[objective]** and authorized scope **[repositories, interfaces, and effects]** for the entire program.

Run three complete sovereign workers in strict sequence:

1. Research owns and repeatedly executes its own plan until its current pass finds no more actionable Research issues.
2. Mastery starts from the changed product and owns and repeatedly executes its own independent plan until its current pass finds no more actionable Mastery issues.

Perfection starts from the changed product and owns exactly 30 complete investigate-plan-execute-check-reinspect loops as one continuous autonomous phase action, working from easy high-leverage failures toward deeper complex completion. A loop counts only after its repairs and checks are recorded internally; internal records do not open another MCP surface or request another user turn.
   - Invoke `solforge_run_perfection_batch` once for the active program. It performs the internal recorder/check cycle and returns only after loop 30 or the first concrete repair failure. Never expand this phase into 30 chat turns or 30 rendered MCP calls.
4. After Perfection loop 30, repeat Research, then Mastery, then exactly 30 new Perfection loops.

Do not combine the worker plans. Do not reduce Research or Mastery to analysis-only handoffs. Do not route every Perfection repair back through them. Preserve each worker's own planning, execution, reinspection, evidence, and convergence rationale inside the larger loop.

Perfection includes an aggressive debugger for failures in the problem-solving machinery. It preserves the interrupted action and last good state, stops faulty mutation, diagnoses the actual command/tool/environment/test/ownership/plan obstruction, repairs its source owner, proves the original action can advance, and resumes under the same approved program. Do not start another approval or outer pass for an ordinary debugging incident.

Use representative product journeys and inspect actual deliverables. Tests, reports, hashes, clean repositories, and plans are supporting evidence only. For each action explain in ordinary English what is broken, exactly what changes, what the user observes, the focused proof, and remaining external uncertainty. Do not manufacture or routinely narrate non-actions; use the accepted effect plan as the landing authority.

The architecture is repository-agnostic. Infer the product type and load only applicable skills. A hardware project target may load PCB/enclosure co-design profiles; other targets must use their own real interfaces and deliverables.

Keep one accepted program and update its durable ledger at every boundary. No further SolForge approval is required for worker iterations or transitions. Direct Pause or Stop applies immediately; Resume continues the last good state. Any Goal is informational only.
