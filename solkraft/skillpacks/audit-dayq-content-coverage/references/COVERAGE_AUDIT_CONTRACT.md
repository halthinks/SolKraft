# DayQ Content Coverage Audit Contract

Audit dimensions:

1. Canon and player-purpose coverage.
2. Catalog/schema/ID/reference integrity.
3. Source, sink, recipe, repair and progression reachability.
4. Family/master/component/variant consistency.
5. Source rights, originality and security.
6. Asset-factory lineage and stage evidence.
7. Blender and Unreal binding completeness.
8. Authority, replication, persistence, migration and anti-duplication.
9. Region/world-placement and spawn plausibility.
10. Performance and density budgets.
11. Accessibility, interaction and presentation standards.
12. Ledger and claim-boundary accuracy.

Severity:

- blocker: corrupts authority, identity, economy, saves, rights, security or required playable route;
- high: required content is unreachable, missing, unproven or materially over budget;
- medium: inconsistent family/state/placement or incomplete secondary evidence;
- low: maintainability, naming or optional coverage issue.

The auditor must preserve failed evidence and never repair production content implicitly.
