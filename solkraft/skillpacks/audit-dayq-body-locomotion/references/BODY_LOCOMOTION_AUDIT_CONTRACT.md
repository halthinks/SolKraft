# Body and Locomotion Audit Contract

Audit:

- ownership and adapter boundaries;
- body rig, regions, collision, Physics Asset, sockets and LODs;
- ground locomotion states, curves, collision, footing and movement exploits;
- Motion Matching, warping, IK, overlays, first-person and proxy presentation;
- wounds, impairment, treatment, incapacity and death handoff;
- impacts, stumbles, ragdolls, recovery, dragging and physics stability;
- clothing, inventory/load, armor, weapons, traversal, weather and exoskeleton integration;
- server authority, prediction, corrections, replay, disconnect and late join;
- persistence, migration, rollback, corruption and vendor removal;
- client/server CPU, GPU, bandwidth, memory and animation/physics LOD;
- keyboard, controller, camera, accessibility and player readability.

Reject evidence generated from prototype static-mesh bodies when the claim concerns production skeletal animation or physics. Reject a clean screenshot as proof of movement feel, authority, persistence or performance.
