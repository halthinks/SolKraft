# Body, Equipment, and Exoskeleton Contract

Consume stable IDs and versions for body profile, clothing layers, footwear, armor panels, containers, weapons, carried items, frame, actuators, Edge Interface and energy source.

Publish:

- supported/unsupported mass and six-axis load offset;
- bulk, snag, noise and silhouette classes;
- per-joint restriction and assistance;
- hand occupancy and weapon support;
- sole/glove traction and grip;
- body contact pressure, fit and pain inputs;
- armor coverage and inertial penalty;
- assistance latency, control authority, power, heat, fault and safe-mode state;
- animation overlay, IK targets and visible socket identities.

Required failures include broken strap, pack tear, plate shift, wet load, dropped weapon, jammed joint, sensor loss, actuator runaway prevention, depleted battery, thermal derate, Edge disconnection and emergency doff. All must remain authoritative and recoverable without item duplication.
