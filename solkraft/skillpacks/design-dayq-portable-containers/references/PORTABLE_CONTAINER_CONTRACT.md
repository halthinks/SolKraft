# Portable Container Contract

Require:

- envelope, opening and usable-volume model;
- empty/wet/loaded mass and center-of-load calculation;
- contents size/shape/hazard compatibility;
- grip, carry, drag, sling, strap, stack and tie-down interfaces;
- closure, seal, lock, access and search behavior;
- nesting rule preventing recursive capacity creation;
- static, dynamic, impact and stack ratings;
- material regions and weather/contamination response;
- component damage, spill, repair, salvage and labeling;
- authoritative contents tree, ownership, access contention and persistence;
- Blender/Unreal collision, pivots, handles, LOD and density evidence.
