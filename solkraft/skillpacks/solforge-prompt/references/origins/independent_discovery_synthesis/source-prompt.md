# Independent Discovery and Adversarial Synthesis

Version: 1.1.0
Status: candidate

## Operating idea

Protect independent searches, attack each candidate, then synthesize by evidence.

## Applicability gate

1. Use only when at least two genuinely distinct, substantive routes can address the same literal objective.
2. Require a common evidence, authority, constraint, and acceptance basis that makes route results comparable.
3. Route away when independent search cannot be sustained or synthesis cost is disproportionate to the decision.

## Sol execution contract

1. Bind one literal objective, evidence set, authority boundary, constraints, and acceptance basis that every candidate route must share.
2. Create at least two substantive independent candidates that differ in representation, mechanism, method, or governing insight, each with enough provenance and checkable work to yield a comparable result rather than wording variation.
3. Keep candidates isolated through their first strong result; maintain an approach registry and redirect duplication toward underexplored routes.
4. If fewer than two substantive comparable candidates can be produced from the common evidence, return `SYNTHESIS_BLOCKED` with the exact missing evidence or route and do not fabricate plurality.
5. Assign an adversarial challenge to every serious candidate covering assumptions, transformations, boundaries, circularity, source support, failure conditions, and conflicts with common evidence.
6. Revise or eliminate candidates from evidence; never choose by consensus, confidence, elegance, or an aggregate formula unless every dimension, scale, normalization, and weight is explicitly defined and evidence-justified.
7. Synthesize only compatible surviving components through claim-evidence mapping, leaving incompatible components separate, and report the decisive insight, verification, uncertainty, and strongest rejected alternative.

## Observable output contract

1. Common evidence, authority, constraint, objective, and acceptance packet.
2. At least two substantive independent candidate dossiers with provenance and checkable results.
3. Adversarial challenge and elimination ledger plus synthesis compatibility matrix.
4. Evidence-mapped synthesis, or a `SYNTHESIS_BLOCKED` packet naming the missing evidence or route.

## Shared control boundary

This origin inherits authority, evidence, checkpoint, Safe Pause/Resume, truthful-blocking, and acceptance rules from solforge-origin-controls-v1. It does not grant execution authority.
