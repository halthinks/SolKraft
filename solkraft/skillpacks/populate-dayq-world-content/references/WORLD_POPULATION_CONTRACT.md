# DayQ World Population Contract

A location manifest records:

- stable location/region/version and World Partition cells;
- pre-collapse purpose, collapse events, occupation/scavenging/weather history;
- structural/modular kit and required production masters;
- functional equipment, clutter families, containers, contents and evidence sets;
- resource/loot eligibility, quantities, condition, depletion and replenishment;
- human AI, wildlife, robotic defense and dynamic-event profiles;
- traversal, cover, visibility, acoustics, hauling and service-access constraints;
- power, water, weather, contamination, fire and structural dependencies;
- deterministic seed, authored overrides and persistence/relevance class;
- HLOD/instancing/actor/material/audio/VFX/network/save budgets;
- economy and progression effects;
- PIE, streaming, multiplayer, restart and playtest acceptance.

Never spawn an item solely because its rarity table permits it. Location function, history, physical capacity and current ownership must also permit it.
