# Proximity Voice, Sound Propagation, and AI Hearing Contract

One coherent acoustic world covers player voice, firearms, footsteps, fire, vehicles, machinery, buildings, weather, wildlife, alarms, radios, AI hearing, stealth, and tactics. Voice transport does not bypass physics except for explicitly equipped radio/intercom/out-of-world modes.

## Foundations and modes

Use Unreal Voice Chat Interface/`IVoiceChat` boundaries; EOS is provisional pending service tests; current Vivox evidence is version-blocked for UE 5.8. Use trusted-server credentials, Sound Attenuation/spatialization/air absorption, Audio Volumes/reverb/submixes/Modulation, MetaSounds/effects, and platform audio. Provider transport never owns DayQ audibility, propagation, radio, privacy, moderation, or AI events.

Support whisper, speech, raised voice, yell, directional local voice, mask/helmet/body-state effects, handheld/worn radio, vehicle intercom, base PA, and only approved equipment/social channels. Each defines input, range/directivity, power/equipment, channel/trust/encryption, signal/noise/interception, occlusion/environment, AI audibility, privacy, and failure.

## Acoustic event and propagation

The server event records stable event/source IDs, position/time/duration, category, source power/frequency/directivity, impulse/continuous, room/surface context, privacy class, and semantic tags. Raw voice content is not sent to AI or ordinary telemetry; AI receives audibility, uncertain origin, duration/category/confidence/recency/masking.

Propagation uses bounded distance, air, terrain/vegetation, walls/floors/doors/windows/openings/shafts/tunnels/caves/bunkers/vehicles, occlusion, room/portal paths, approximate diffraction/transmission/reflections/reverb, elevation/weather/masking, listener orientation, headgear/hearing damage/protection, and firearm blast/action/crack/impact. Use relevance, cached graphs, significance, event classes, and budgets rather than unlimited traces.

## Privacy, radios, AI, and health

The trusted server authorizes recipients, channel membership, position/range, radio ownership/frequency/key/mode, mute/block, death/spectator, and moderation hooks. Unauthorized clients receive no local voice frames. Record consent/retention/reporting/parental/platform/accessibility/STT/TTS policy before adoption; no raw storage by default.

Radio propagation is separate from acoustics and integrates inventory, power/batteries, antennas, terrain/elevation/structures/weather, damage/repair, frequencies/trust, scanners/repeaters/abstract countermeasures, vehicles/bases/exos/mechs/defenses, and persistence. AI uses uncertain evidence and memory to investigate/alert/communicate/search/retreat/ignore; never exact wall-through locations. Hearing damage/protection is optional and decision-gated, accessible, and bounded.
