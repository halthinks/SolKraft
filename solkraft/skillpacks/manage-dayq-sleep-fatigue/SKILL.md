Read [FULL_CONTRACT.md](references/FULL_CONTRACT.md) and [INTEGRATION.md](references/INTEGRATION.md). Extend existing survival, health, inventory, bases, movement, combat, audio, fire, clothing, vehicles, exo/mech, authority, and persistence owners.
