# Serial Research, Mastery, and Perfection contract

## One outer cycle, three sovereign workers

Every autonomous outer cycle contains three independently created plan streams:

1. **Code Research plans** investigate explicit, inferred, and latent intent; map evidence, inconsistencies, missing behavior, hidden capability, and research-supported remediation; execute every Research repair; check the result; and repeat until Research converges.
2. **Code Mastery plans** independently investigate the product after Research converges; determine and execute the implementation, integration, release-readiness, focused verification, and landing work; check the result; and repeat until Mastery converges.
3. **Perfection plans** independently investigate, plan, execute, check, and reinspect the current product exactly thirty times after Mastery converges.

The loop compiler never combines these plans. It stores and displays only the active worker's plan, preserves completed worker artifacts as history, and leaves a future worker's plan absent until that worker independently investigates and records it. A saved finding or plan is never mislabeled as an executed or converged worker pass.

## Executable route records

Every sovereign worker-plan action records:

- stable action identifier and source worker;
- ordinary-language action and reason;
- selected skill and why it was selected;
- callable MCP or built-in tool route;
- exact inputs or linked input artifact;
- expected outputs and stored result path;
- prerequisites and downstream dependents;
- focused completion signal;
- remaining uncertainty; and
- current state: planned, running, completed, blocked, or skipped as unnecessary.

The durable iteration plan links the readable and structured plan files and names the active worker's ordered callable route. An optional verbose Goal may summarize this plan but has no control authority.

## Approval and continuation

The user accepts the outer loop once. That acceptance launches the approved program directly. Later worker passes and outer cycles update the durable iteration plan and automatically refresh the Loop Observer without another Strategy approval. Direct Pause, Stop, and Resume remain available at every durable boundary.

## Truthful recording

SolForge derives changed files from fresh repository state. Optional caller descriptions may enrich actual changed files but may not define them. Approved paths that were inspected and required no change are recorded as reviewed unchanged. Missing fake mutations, old fingerprints, or formatting-only evidence never block the next cycle.
