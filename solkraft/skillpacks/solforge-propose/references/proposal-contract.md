# Proposal contract

The proposal capsule binds the original Prompt capsule, exact prompt result, Codebase capsule, repository fingerprint, codebase-result hash, evidence-ledger hash, user-selected proposal direction, steering history, capsule-proposal receipt, capsule-creation receipt, and the complete execution-quality contract.

The plan artifact must contain:

- original intent and current decision;
- verified findings and their evidence locators;
- contrary evidence and unresolved uncertainty;
- alternatives considered and why each was selected, rejected, or deferred;
- ordered atomic work units with prerequisites and pause boundaries;
- exact files, repositories, data, tools, and external systems in scope;
- permissions and mutations required per unit;
- proportional verification, rollback, budgets, and stop conditions;
- explicit testing depth, validation independence, adversarial intensity, evidence depth, rollback proof, and checkpoint granularity;
- the allowed execution strategies and one preferred strategy;
- requirement-level acceptance criteria;
- supported specialized Run routes.

The proposal may narrow a recommended execution path based on evidence, but it may not delete an original material requirement, conceal disagreement, invent authority, treat proposal as capsule creation, treat creation as execution approval, or permit a later execution route to weaken the locked quality contract.
