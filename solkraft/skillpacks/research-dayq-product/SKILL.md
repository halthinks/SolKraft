# DayQ Product Research

Use current primary sources wherever available and distinguish sourced facts from inference.

Read [research-contract.md](references/research-contract.md). Browse because platform, licensing, market, certification, service, and legal information changes.

## Workflow

1. Define the decision the research must inform and the date/market/platform scope.
2. Prefer official platform, engine, regulator, publisher, storefront, and company documentation. Use reputable market evidence when primary data is unavailable.
3. Triangulate estimates; expose model assumptions and uncertainty.
4. Separate minimum credible, target, and full-vision implications.
5. Identify design consequences for platform, session, server, moderation, content, privacy, accessibility, localization, rating, and legal/IP choices.
6. Record sources next to supported claims and state which conclusions are inference.
7. Feed conclusions back into decision records; do not silently override creative direction.

## Scope rule

Staffing and budgets are not the primary objective, but lifecycle costs and operational obligations must still be documented when they affect feasibility, architecture, commercial model, moderation, or certification.

## Output

Deliver a dated evidence table, analysis, uncertainty, recommendation, design consequences, and decision records to update.
