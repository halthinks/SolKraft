# Product research contract

Required topics:

- audience size and comparable games;
- target price and sales range;
- platform certification;
- engine licensing;
- server and live-operations constraints;
- moderation and customer support;
- content cadence;
- team-size and outsourcing implications;
- marketing;
- localization and accessibility;
- content rating and legal exposure;
- security, analytics, anti-cheat, platform fees, updates, and community management.

For each topic record question, date, region/platform, primary sources, secondary evidence if needed, facts, inference, uncertainty, minimum/target/full-vision implications, recommendation, and affected decisions.

Avoid direct appropriation of protected names, brands, living individuals, or real ethnic/national groups as fictional villains. Preserve geopolitical causality through fictionalized actors and obtain jurisdiction-specific counsel before relying on legal conclusions.
