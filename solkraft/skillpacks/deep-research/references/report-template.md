# Deep Research Report Template

## Executive Answer

Give the direct answer in 3-7 bullets. Mark each bullet as fact, inference, or recommendation.

## Scope And Method

- Research question:
- Decision this supports:
- Date researched:
- Tools used:
- Important assumptions:

## Source Ledger

Summarize the strongest sources first. Include source IDs from `sources.csv`.

| ID | Source | Type | Quality | Relevance | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Claim Matrix

Every important conclusion needs evidence IDs.

| Claim | Status | Confidence | Evidence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Findings

Group findings by theme. Separate direct source-backed facts from synthesis.

## Contradictions And Gaps

List conflicts between sources, missing data, failed searches, unknowns, and what would change the answer.

## Local Repo Impact

Tie research back to local files, code paths, docs, configs, scripts, model artifacts, or tests.

## Recommendations

Rank by impact and confidence. Include what to do next, what to avoid claiming, and what should be measured.

## Appendix

Add search queries, local commands, benchmark notes, and short compliant quotes.
