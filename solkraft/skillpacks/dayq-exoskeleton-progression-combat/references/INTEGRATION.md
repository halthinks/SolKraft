# Integration ownership

Compose around existing inventory/equipment, crafting, movement, traversal, combat/damage, power, AI, multiplayer authority, and persistence systems. Do not duplicate their state. Confirm the conceptual decision that exoskeletons appear early and remain core to combat and progression.

## Clothing adapter

`build-dayq-clothing` owns exoskeleton underlayers, harness interfaces, cooling-garment regions, seals, cable clearances, fit, chafing, localized connector damage, and emergency-release compatibility. This skill owns frame hardpoints, actuators, supported load, power, heat, movement, calibration, and combat. Compatibility is an explicit versioned interface; no hidden armor/insulation/load stacking is allowed.
