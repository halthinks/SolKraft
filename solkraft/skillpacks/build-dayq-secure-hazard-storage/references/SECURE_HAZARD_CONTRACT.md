# Secure and Hazard Storage Contract

Record threat/hazard classes, construction, seal/closure, lock/authentication, ventilation/insulation/shielding, sensors, power, compatible contents, segregation, safe capacity, access policy, audit, breach, emergency behavior, contamination, fire/thermal response, repair and persistent state.

Ammo cans organize and protect ammunition; they do not make unstable or damaged ammunition safe. Gas cylinders require upright restraint and compatible cages. Battery storage requires chemistry-aware isolation/monitoring through the power/fire owners. Avoid real-world procedural instructions for weapon or explosive manufacture.
