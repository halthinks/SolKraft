# DayQ canon

## Timeline

- Within 15 days, order deteriorated to living-dead-level panic, violence, displacement, and infrastructure failure without literal undead.
- By day 30, food, fuel, medicine, transport, and authority had fragmented into a Mad Max-like warlord world.
- For three years, Chinese state actors enabled South American cartel forces and proxies to assault American territory while defensive resources formed survival parties, war parties, clans, and warlords. Production fiction should use fictionalized names while preserving the causal premise.
- A failed Chinese takedown was thwarted by a doomsday quantum-cloud delivery system operating through clandestine sovereign quantum communications and development infrastructure hidden behind proxy companies aligned with U.S. defensive interests.
- The network was assumed impossible because stable quantum applications were not believed able to sustain useful links with traditional infrastructure.
- It was an air-gapped dead-man's switch outside Hearthline. Hearthline agents breached it and triggered quantum decryption against global finance, identity, command, and cryptography not protected by on-chip post-quantum hardware roots of trust.
- Only a small contingency had begun installing that hardware. Authentication failure, false orders, asset seizure, logistics collapse, sabotage, retaliation, and lockout caused global fallout with no winner.
- Twenty-eight years have passed. Some elites remain unreachable in sealed caves and bunkers; other elite communities are empty but still protected by autonomous post-quantum grids.

## Core arc

Leave base -> scavenge -> survive -> extract -> build and industrialize -> analyze strongholds -> fabricate target-specific suppression -> raid elite enclaves -> capture hardware/infrastructure -> prepare bunker operations -> occupy and defend.

## Technology rules

- Post-quantum security depends on physical chips, keys, sensors, actuators, isolated networks, and power.
- Quantum-field suppression is fictional but local, temporary, target-specific, energy intensive, detectable, failure-prone, and countered by shielding, redundancy, interception, and miscalibration.
- Heavy delivery drones require lift, navigation, control, payload cooling, power, and recovery or planned loss.
- Strategic raids require intelligence and logistics, never a universal crafting-menu hack.
