# Locked DayQ Energy and Industry Contract

## Decision

Status: `decided` by the user on 2026-07-19. The selected design is `manufactured-storage-stirling-spiral`.

The gameplay direction is locked. Exact quantities, rates, yields, timings, capacities, power ratings, losses, loot frequency, and balance remain evidence-gated variables.

## Player-facing spiral

1. Locate predictable dead-battery donor ecosystems.
2. Extract reusable modules and reclaim graded material lots.
3. Manufacture an initially empty B0 work battery at the Field Battery Bench.
4. Earn its first charge through human power, solar, wind, water, temporary facility/vehicle access, trade, theft, or cooperation.
5. Spend scarce charge to wake the Edge agent and restore a damaged P0/P1 fabricator plus the concise F1 wheel forge.
6. Inspect actual donor machines, choose what to sacrifice, and let the agent propose bounded substitutions.
7. Manufacture an LS0 external-heat Linear Stirling and a compact plastic-conversion plant.
8. Convert prepared compatible plastic into crude burner oil; process gas is a station-efficiency credit and char/residue is a real loss/byproduct.
9. Run LS0, charge B1, and operate the first sustained workshop.
10. Upgrade the plastic-fuel family to produce engine-compatible synthetic gasoline without a carried fraction-chain.
11. Use synthetic gasoline in repaired municipal generators for loud, fast, high-output production windows.
12. Use sustained plus peak power for controlled metalworking, higher Glentech capability, water treatment, gas handling, recovered-vessel use, later cylinder manufacture, and surplus-electricity electrolysis.
13. Use fuel-cell/battery hybrids for quiet portable endurance in radios, relays, drones, exoskeletons, agents, suppression systems, and mechs.

## Mandatory donor ecosystems

All are distinct predictable world sources:

- forklift and warehouse-vehicle traction packs;
- telecom backup cabinets;
- UPS and data-center racks;
- solar-storage installations;
- electric and hybrid vehicles;
- hospital emergency systems;
- industrial robots and Battle Circuit workshops;
- rare defended residential wall packs.

Dead batteries remain repair, reusable-cell/module, reclaimed-material, and trade resources. Restoration versus destructive reclamation is irreversible and server-authoritative.

## Battery progression

- **B0 Rebuilt work cell:** lead-acid/lead-carbon route; black start, portable electronics, controls, and one critical P0/P1 job.
- **B1 Workshop lead bank:** improved buswork, enclosure, protection, ventilation, and control; Stirling buffer and medium workshop surges.
- **B2 Rugged stationary bank:** lead-carbon, industrial lead-acid, or nickel-iron; long-lived base and renewable storage.
- **B3 Rebuilt lithium module:** graded recovered cells plus new enclosure, buswork, cooling, isolation, and BMS; drones, tools, vehicles, and early exos.
- **B4 Advanced manufactured pack:** controlled cell/material production or qualified recovered cells; combat exos, high-performance drones, and mobile compute.
- **B5 Sovereign hybrid storage:** advanced cells, fuel cells, transient storage, hardened control, and secure interfaces; mechs, quantum systems, and bunker operations.

Battery chemistry is a mission choice:

- lead-acid/lead-carbon: common, repairable, high surge, heavy;
- nickel-iron: durable and tolerant, bulky, inefficient, slow;
- recovered lithium: light and powerful, inspection-intensive and thermally vulnerable;
- advanced manufactured cells: high performance with controlled-production dependency;
- battery/fuel-cell hybrid: endurance plus transient output at high complexity.

Track capacity, voltage/output class, charge, condition, cycle wear, internal-resistance abstraction, management state, mass, dimensions, thermal/leak risk, ownership, and provenance. Large packs require physical hauling and cannot enter an ordinary backpack.

## LS0-LS4 Linear Stirling family

Every LS machine is external-heat with a sealed nitrogen working gas. Nitrogen is not fuel.

- **LS0 Experimental power head:** P0/P1 repair/jigs, F1 rough stock, B0 controls, recovered precision donors; intermittent low output and frequent tuning.
- **LS1 Field generator:** P2, R2, F2, standardized components; reliable small-base load and B1 charging.
- **LS2 Workshop generator:** P3/P4, R3, F3, improved inspection and agent simulation; continuous workshop power and useful heat recovery.
- **LS3 Base CHP bank:** P5, R4, F4, industrial installation; synchronized modules, load sharing, settlement base load, industrial heat, and microgrid support.
- **LS4 Heavy Stirling plant:** P6/P7, R5, F5, endgame G/M capability and strategic logistics; war industry, major compute, hydrogen support, and mech logistics.

A printer tier is necessary but never sufficient. Matching reclamation, forge/foundry, materials, metrology, base, machine, power, cooling, installation, and agent capability are mandatory. Configurations may favor efficiency, field service, reduced signature, CHP, industrial heat, redundancy, transportability, or waste-heat recovery without bypassing tier limits.

## Fuel progression

The compact converter consumes prepared compatible plastic, process heat, limited control power, cooling, and collection capacity. The first batch can start without LS0. Suitable feedstock yields crude burner oil; contaminated feedstock changes yield, fouling, waste, signature, and wear.

Crude burner oil is valid external process heat and LS fuel. It is never gasoline and is rejected by ordinary gasoline generators.

A substantial physical plant upgrade or workshop-scale replacement adds the required installed capabilities for one player-facing synthetic-gasoline job:

`prepared compatible plastic + process consumables + power/heat -> synthetic gasoline + waste`

Fuel lots track consumer class, energy band, fouling/contamination risk, stability, quantity, container, ownership, and provenance. No mandatory carried intermediate-fraction parade is allowed.

## Power roles and network

- Battery: silent reserve, black start, transient/surge buffer.
- Stirling: slow-response steady workshop/base load and useful waste heat.
- Municipal internal combustion: expensive high-output peak load, fast response, high signature.
- Solar/wind/water: geographic and intermittent contribution requiring storage/control.
- Hydrogen/fuel cell: lossy but quiet portable endurance, paired with batteries for transient power.

The physical network includes DC/AC buses, charge controllers, inverters, protection/isolation, named circuits, priority loads, synchronization, load shedding, hidden emergency circuits, weather exposure, damage, sabotage, repair, Edge monitoring, and agent scheduling.

Electricity is operational ammunition. Base survival, water, food, medicine, communications, defenses, manufacturing, agent compute, drones, exos, suppression preparation, raids, and reserve compete for the same finite supply.

## Waste heat and warfare

Waste heat can dry clothing/equipment, heat shelters/water/greenhouses/medical spaces, condition batteries, and support suitable processes. It also creates infrared signature, fire/ventilation load, heat stress, exhaust, condensation, and snowmelt.

Power warfare includes exposed generation/distribution attack, battery or generator theft, cooling/charge-control damage, bounded sabotage, fuel-delivery interruption, renewable-site capture, signature detection, and forcing load-allocation choices. Existing raid-window, offline-protection, repair, and recovery owners remain authoritative.

## Agent and progression boundaries

Agents inspect actual inventory, diagnose, propose 2-3 bounded adaptations where appropriate, predict faults/runtime, schedule loads, and optimize later systems. They never create resources, assert certainty without evidence, bypass workstations, or silently certify unknown components.

All origins can complete the core chain and train relevant trees through T5. Native T6 may provide exceptional designs or tactics but never gates a core battery, LS generator, fuel, hydrogen, or campaign objective.

## Hydrogen

Recovered compatible vessels precede new manufacture. Later new vessels require controlled metal stock, forming, heat treatment, machining/finishing, inspection, power, and material handling, compressed into one readable game job without certificate/serialization bureaucracy.

Electrolysis requires purified water, an electrolyzer and balance-of-plant abstractions, sustained or surplus electricity, drying/gas handling, compatible storage, and later compression/manufactured vessels. A battery may black-start controls but is not the energy source for a useful hydrogen economy.

Hydrogen never multiplies energy. It converts expensive stationary generation into quiet portable endurance.
