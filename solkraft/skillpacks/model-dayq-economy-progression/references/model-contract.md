# Quantitative model contract

## Economy variables

Item spawn rates, resource consumption, repair costs, death losses, construction costs, storage growth, salvage return, average clan income, and rare-component circulation.

## Progression outputs

Hours to camp, outpost, settlement, industrial stronghold, suppression technology, and bunker readiness for solo, four-person, twelve-person, and organized-large-clan cohorts.

## Server-capacity inputs

Concurrent players, bases, placeables, vehicles, AI, loot, physics, network traffic, database writes, and world-state size.

## Required validation

- dimensional/unit checks;
- formula and code review;
- at least one independent spot calculation;
- seed and scenario reproducibility;
- baseline, optimistic, pessimistic, and exploit scenarios;
- sensitivity ranking;
- assumptions and limitations.
