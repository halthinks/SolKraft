# DayQ Asset Family Contract

A family records:

- stable family ID and version;
- catalog entries served and variants intentionally excluded;
- invariant physical identity and construction;
- production masters and selection rules;
- parameter definitions with units, bounds and discretization;
- components, slots, compatibility keys and mutual exclusions;
- shared skeleton/rig, socket, pivot, collision and interaction standards;
- material regions and state families;
- damage, repair, missing-part and salvage configurations;
- faction/canon deltas;
- inventory, hauling, installation and building interfaces;
- recipe, workstation, progression and economy dependencies;
- Unreal class/Data Asset/Primary Asset mapping;
- authority, replication, persistence and migration mapping;
- LOD, texture, material, physics, animation, audio, VFX and density budgets;
- representative combination tests.

Do not create a variant merely by multiplying every option. Generate only combinations that are physically compatible, canonically plausible, gameplay-relevant, visually distinguishable, reachable in progression and within production/runtime budgets.
