# System decision-sheet contract

Required systems: combat, death, inventory, survival, health, radiation/contamination, scavenging, loot, crafting, building, power, water, food, medicine, vehicles, clans, territory, faction reputation, AI, raids, bunkers, defense suppression, economy, and persistence.

Every sheet must contain:

1. Player purpose and intended feeling.
2. Design goals and explicit non-goals.
3. State model and units.
4. Inputs and acquisition sources.
5. Rules and update cadence.
6. Outputs and feedback.
7. Failure, recovery, and irreversible consequences.
8. Progression and difficulty scaling.
9. Cross-system dependencies.
10. Multiplayer authority, permissions, replication, and persistence.
11. Exploits and mitigations.
12. Telemetry.
13. Unresolved questions.
14. Prototype required.
15. Acceptance criteria.
16. Decision status and evidence links.

Use tables only when they clarify exact mappings. Quantify thresholds or ranges; label uncertainty instead of inventing precision.
