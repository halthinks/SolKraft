# DayQ Image-to-Production Asset

Use procedural reconstruction for fast, reviewable truth-finding and Blender for production art. Never treat a single image as proof of exact hidden geometry.

Read [asset-route.md](references/asset-route.md). If no immutable asset-factory job exists, invoke `$build-dayq-systemic-asset-factory` exactly once to create and route it. When a factory job already exists, do not invoke the factory again; act only as its reconstruction and Blender-promotion stage. Follow the factory's provenance, environmental, img2threejs, Blender-repair, Unreal-acceptance, and job-lineage contracts. Read the project schemas before finalizing a contract.

## Workflow

1. Create the asset-factory job only when no job context exists; otherwise verify and reuse the current immutable job. Receive a canon-bound gameplay brief and classify reference quality, asset risk, licensing, security, quarantine, and hidden-geometry uncertainty.
2. Inventory identity-defining details, scale cues, hierarchy, materials, wear, interactions, components, repair, salvage, damage, sockets, pivots, and colliders.
3. Define every environmental channel and connect shelter/exposure and material-region state to physical, operational, repair, salvage, replication, and persistence consequences.
4. Record source identity, rights, attribution, hash, quarantine, and security disposition; reject unresolved rights or unsafe content.
5. Write a schema-valid V2 asset contract and job manifest.
6. When suitable, run the current img2threejs sequence: suitability, detail inventory, ObjectSculptSpec, strict gate, locked blockout/structural/form/material/surface/lighting/interaction/optimization passes.
7. Render fixed comparison views at every pass; record scores and exactly one decision among continue, refine-spec, refine-code, request-input, or stop; do not advance without evidence.
8. Promote through Blender MCP and repeat inspect → defect → repair → rerender until production acceptance or a truthful block.
9. Integrate through the DayQ Unreal MCP production skill and validate scale, interaction, environment, navigation, collision, destruction, repair, salvage, performance, replication, persistence, crash recovery, and regressions.

Do not hand-author a second mesh during promotion. Export the accepted procedural `THREE.Group` as a hashed GLB when it is already correct. When visual review requires Blender-side geometric correction, record the correction as declarative governed parts in the job production spec and let `dayq_blender_promoter.py` interpret them. Asset-specific coordinates belong only to the reviewed job spec, never to the reusable promoter. Reject direct GLB promotion when comparison proves the procedural silhouette inadequate; preserve the rejection, refine the spec/model, and rerun.

Prefer procedural reconstruction for hard-surface props, modular kits, cabinets, machinery, drones, and blockout vehicles. Go directly to Blender or a dedicated pipeline for humans, organic creatures, cloth, exact mechanical interiors, or cinematic deformation.

Deliver the unified job manifest, source/provenance/security record, brief, environmental model, spec, stage renders, discrepancy log, V2 asset contract, Blender issues/repairs/inspection renders, exports, Unreal tests/evidence, and validation report. Mark live-tool gaps blocked rather than passed.
