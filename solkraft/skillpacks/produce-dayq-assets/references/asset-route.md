# DayQ asset route

| Route | Best for | Avoid for |
|---|---|---|
| Procedural Three.js -> Blender | hard-surface props, modular kits, machinery, drones, blockouts | organic characters, cloth, exact internals |
| Direct Blender | complex production assets, deformation, rigs, final vehicles | rapid disposable shape tests |
| Licensed source -> Blender | common realistic objects with documented provenance | unclear licenses or wrong identity |

Stage gates: blockout; structure; form; materials; surface/story; representative lighting; interaction/repair/destruction; optimization. Preserve fixed cameras and record whether the specification or implementation caused each discrepancy.
