# Fixed Storage Contract

Record footprint, height, bays, openings, shelf/grid geometry, load ratings, anchoring, access/aisle volumes, container/bin compatibility, permissions, security, environment, structural failure, repair and salvage.

Overloaded storage must fail causally. It may deform, tip, shed a shelf, jam a drawer or collapse a bay; it may not silently delete contents. Contents moves/spills remain atomic inventory transactions.
