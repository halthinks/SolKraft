# DayQ Rig and Interaction Contract

Required fields:

- stable standard ID/version and applicable asset/actor families;
- gameplay owner and authority boundary;
- coordinate, units, axes, scale and naming convention;
- hierarchy, bones/pivots, constraints, degrees of freedom and limits;
- sockets, attachment types, compatibility keys and occupancy;
- interaction state machine, prerequisites, phases, commits, cancellation and recovery;
- stance/approach volumes, hands/tools, load/reach/strength and obstruction;
- animation montage/events, IK, motion warping and procedural boundaries;
- collision, physics, damage and detachable-part mapping;
- replication, prediction, correction, contention, reconnect and persistence;
- Blender export and Unreal import/conformance tests;
- accessibility/controller and first/third-person presentation requirements.

Never let an animation notify author inventory transfer, damage, crafting completion or persistent state. It may request an authoritative commit from the owning system.
