# DayQ Game-Ordnance Contract

## Safety and fiction boundary

This is a game-design and Unreal-production contract. It must not contain real explosive manufacture, synthesis, proportions, charge weights, pressure-vessel design, wiring diagrams, initiation circuits, timer construction, concealment guidance, or procedures for modifying live ammunition. Use fictional recipe tokens such as `energetic_package`, `control_module`, `safe_fuze_unit`, `fragmentation_body`, and `mounting_kit`. These are game inventory classes, not material substitutions or instructions.

## Item contract

Every ordnance identity defines category, found-versus-crafted source, rarity, carried load and slot shape, compatible launcher/mount, authority state, safe/armed/placed/triggered/spent/dud/damaged states, delivery envelope, effect channels, environment response, AI/sound consequences, counterplay, repair/disposal rule, persistence/tombstone behavior, economy source/sink, asset/VFX/audio route, and acceptance tests.

## Recipe contract

Crafted ordnance uses abstract component IDs, minimum grades, station tier, tools as gameplay classes, power, work duration, player skill, agent-assistance ceiling, batch cap, quality output, reliability, detectability, failure states, and conservation. The UI communicates capability and tradeoffs without exposing real construction knowledge.

## Gameplay rule

Military service ordnance is scarce, degraded, and generally found, captured, traded, or recovered from defended stores. Player manufacturing produces fictional DayQ devices with distinct balance and appearance. High-tier systems require clan infrastructure and leave strategic signatures; they are never pocket-factory conveniences.
