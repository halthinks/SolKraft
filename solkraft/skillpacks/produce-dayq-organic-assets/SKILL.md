# Produce DayQ Organic Assets

Use a dedicated deformable pipeline; never force organic work through the rigid reconstruction route.

Read [ORGANIC_PRODUCTION_CONTRACT.md](references/ORGANIC_PRODUCTION_CONTRACT.md). Inspect canon, body/skeleton standards, clothing, inventory/load, health/injury, AI, traversal, combat, animation, material-state, LOD and Unreal authority contracts.

## Workflow

1. Classify human, wearable/soft good, animal/creature, injury/morph, hair/groom or deformable prop.
2. Establish ethical/source provenance, originality, identity/privacy and rating constraints.
3. Define anatomy, proportions, silhouette families, size/fit ranges, skeletal standard, deformation requirements and gameplay hit/coverage regions.
4. Produce concept/reference turnarounds with hidden-surface uncertainty; do not infer unsupported anatomy from one image.
5. Create high/low topology, UVs, textures/material regions, skeleton/skin, corrective shapes, cloth/groom setup and physics proxies.
6. Bind clothing, armor, backpacks and equipment through approved fit/attachment standards; test extreme poses and size classes.
7. Define injury, wetness, contamination, damage, repair/cleaning and corpse/degradation states where appropriate.
8. Build LODs, animation budget, crowd variants and distant representation.
9. Integrate through versioned Unreal staging, existing gameplay owners, PIE, multiplayer, persistence and representative performance tests.
10. Preserve one asset-factory lineage even when specialist external tools are used.

## Completion

Deliver provenance, concepts, production meshes, skeleton/skin/cloth/groom, material regions, fit matrices, injury/state sets, LODs, collisions, animation conformance, Unreal bindings, performance and evidence.
