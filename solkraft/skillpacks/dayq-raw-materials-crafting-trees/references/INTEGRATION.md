# Integration ownership

Extend existing crafting, building, economy/progression, power, authoritative inventory, and persistence systems. Do not create parallel item identities, transactions, construction states, journals, or migrations. Treat a shift from abstract recipes to material-grade industry as a conceptual change requiring confirmation.

## Clothing adapter

`build-dayq-clothing` owns garment patterns, panels, seams, closures, localized garment function, fit, and repair intent. This skill owns material lots, grades, reservations, workstation capabilities, byproducts, conservation, and salvage transformation. A clothing job references existing lot/item IDs and commits through the existing transaction/journal boundary; neither side may duplicate materials or invent a separate recipe authority.

## Power-economy adapter

Recovered municipal generators are existing persistent items repaired through crafting jobs and consumed by the power owner. Reclaimed petro-fuel is a graded lot from the circular-energy chain. Residential wall packs retain one unique item identity through raid access, isolation, diagnosis, lift/vehicle transfer, installation, repair, ownership, and persistence. This skill supplies repair/material recipes only; it never creates a second generator, fuel, battery, inventory, raid, power-flow, or journal authority.
