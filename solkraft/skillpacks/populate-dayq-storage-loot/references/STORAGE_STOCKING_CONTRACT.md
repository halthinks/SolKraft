# Storage Stocking Contract

Each profile records location/function/history, eligible storage families, eligible contents, capacity/shape/hazard checks, organization, quantities, condition/provenance, lock/access, prior depletion, deterministic seed, authored overrides, NPC logistics, replenishment, persistence and economy caps.

A hospital cabinet, municipal tool locker, household plastic tote, military ammo can and elite-vault drawer must not share a generic loot pool.
