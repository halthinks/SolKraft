# CAD-bound render lineage

## Proof render

A proof render must record:

- master STEP path and SHA-256;
- source mesh path and SHA-256 for every visible product part;
- exporter and renderer versions;
- unit scale, object transforms, camera, resolution, and material-only overrides;
- output image SHA-256;
- configuration name and visible/hidden parts.

Do not permit silhouette edits after mesh export. Context geometry must be separately named and excluded from product-fit claims unless it is itself a controlled collision fixture.

## Presentation study

Photographic, generated, composited, or retouched imagery may explore materials, lighting, framing, environment, and customer context. Mark it `PRESENTATION_ONLY`. Compare it against proof views using front, side, rear, aperture-count, seam, control, mount, and accessory-interface gates.

Reject a study if it:

- turns distinct service masses into one uniform brick;
- changes the number or location of apertures, controls, clamps, rails, or pods;
- moves the optic or changes the mounting datum;
- invents vents, connectors, display geometry, or impossible assembly seams;
- hides packaging conflicts behind perspective, darkness, or crop.

High-end appearance comes from credible materials, controlled edge hierarchy, realistic scale cues, precise fit lines, restrained markings, and context—not from changing the physical design.
