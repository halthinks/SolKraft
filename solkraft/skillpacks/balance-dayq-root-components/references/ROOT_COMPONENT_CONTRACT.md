# Root component and reclamation contract

## Material families

Ferrous; light structural metal; conductive metal; thermoplastic; elastomer/seal; resin/adhesive; fiber reinforcement; glass/optical; ceramic/refractory; electronic substrate/printable conductor; chemical/process consumables; energy/electrochemical.

Every lot records family/subtype, grade/form, mass/dimensions, purity, condition, contamination/moisture/corrosion/fatigue, provenance, hazard/storage, accepted processes, recovery yield, and loss.

## Standardized part families

Fasteners/inserts; pins/clips/hinges; bearings/guides; shafts/gears/drives; springs/dampers; seals/couplings; tubes/hoses/fittings; valves/manifolds; pumps/compressors; thermal modules; wire/cable/fiber; connectors/backplanes; passive/protection electronics; power conversion; logic/controllers; memory/storage; radio/network; sensors; displays/controls; motors/actuators; cells/fuel cells; optics/emitters; structures/enclosures; trusted compute.

Use Common, Technical, Precision, Secure, and Sovereign rarity inside families.

## R0-R5

- R0 hand salvage.
- R1 basic reclamation with concise Wheel Forge and polymer preparation.
- R2 controlled batches and separated feedstock.
- R3 tested printer-grade materials and conductors.
- R4 advanced alloy/composite/electronic qualification.
- R5 strategic high-performance material qualification.

No printer consumes generic raw scrap. Precious-metal yields remain small, lossy, and equipment-gated. Plastic is sorted, cleaned, shredded, dried, and recompounded into bricks, pellets, rods, or spools; it is not forged as metal.

## Fairness and integrity

Ordinary inputs need at least two of targeted salvage, careful deconstruction, repair/remanufacture, additive/conventional manufacture, trade, contracts, captured output, substitution, or printed stopgap. Mass in equals product plus byproduct, waste, contamination, and declared loss. Server owns reservations, transformation, outputs, and provenance.
