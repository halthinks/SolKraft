# Storage Universe Contract

Every storage family records stable ID/version, category, physical construction, exterior/internal dimensions, openings, volume, load/stack ratings, access class, placement/mobility, security/seal/insulation/shielding, contents compatibility, components, damage/repair/salvage, progression, spawning, stocking, production masters, runtime owner and evidence.

Required families include soft pouches and sacks; backpacks and harnesses; duffels and suitcases; toolboxes and hard cases; ammo cans and magazines; plastic totes and bins; crates, pallets and drums; coolers and dry boxes; lockers and cabinets; drawers and parts bins; shelves and pallet racks; weapon/tool racks; safes and vault containers; medical, food, battery, chemical, cylinder and cold storage; vehicle, drone, exo and mech cargo.

Variants share a family only when opening, load path, components, interaction and gameplay remain compatible.
