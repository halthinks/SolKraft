# Motion and Animation Contract

Require:

- stable animation-profile and skeleton versions;
- vendor-neutral movement trajectory and context tags;
- Pose Search schemas, databases, LODs, query channels, weights and deterministic debug capture;
- Chooser rules for stance, gait, direction, weapon posture, load, injury, assistance and action;
- start/stop/pivot/turn/fall/land/stance-transition coverage;
- separate upper-body overlays and hand-occupancy rules;
- root-motion and Motion-Warping authorization contract;
- foot, pelvis, hand, look and weapon-support IK with contact-quality limits;
- physical-reaction and ragdoll blending interfaces;
- first-person arm/body policy and camera-motion accessibility controls;
- proxy/crowd animation LOD, update-rate and Pose Search budgets;
- retargeting, montage, notify filtering, frame-rate, correction and network conformance tests.

The Game Animation Sample can supply learnable/migratable reference content after license verification, but its example traversal logic cannot replace DayQ's authoritative traversal rules.
