# Edge Interface doctrine

The Edge Interface is the player's persistent digital survival object. It is not merely a menu skin and not automatically granted at spawn.

Use an original DayQ industrial name and visual language. “Cell-phone-like” describes portability and daily importance. “Dolphin-class” describes a modular field radio/electronics/security interface, not a copied commercial device or mascot.

## Condition bands

- **Ruined shell:** useful only for salvage or data recovery.
- **Severely damaged:** intermittent boot, broken display/input, weak radio, poor sealing, degraded storage or battery, possible compromise.
- **Field-repairable:** one or more major modules missing or faulty; limited local agent and communications capability.
- **Serviceable:** reliable core functions with ordinary wear and repair history.
- **Refurbished:** tested modules, new seals, calibrated radios, secure firmware, known provenance.
- **Like-new/recovered secure:** high condition, full environmental rating, intact trusted hardware where installed.
- **Clan-built:** modular domestically manufactured frame using standardized DayQ parts.
- **Sovereign/elite:** rare hardened compute, radios, storage, secure roots, shielding, and advanced agent capability.

Condition is component-specific. A pristine screen does not imply trusted firmware; a damaged enclosure does not automatically destroy stored checkpoints. Persist every critical component and trust state.

Create a versioned target-security profile rather than a single lock level:

```yaml
security_profile:
  target_id: stable_id
  physical_security_tier: 0..6
  identity_authentication_tier: 0..6
  software_crypto_tier: legacy|modern_classical|hybrid_pqc|pqc
  key_storage: exposed_software|sealed_software|secure_element|certified_hardware_root
  secure_boot: none|software|hardware_rooted
  firmware_condition: {}
  segmentation: {}
  monitoring_detection: {}
  rate_limit_lockout: {}
  biometric_factor: none|single|multi
  recovery_paths: []
  connected_weak_links: []
  quantum_direct_path: open|staged|closed_by_hardware_root
  current_compromise: {}
```

Recommended bands:

- **S0 Mechanical/none:** no cyber route unless a connected controller exists.
- **S1 Legacy digital:** common credentials, exposed buses, weak firmware, low monitoring.
- **S2 Hardened classical:** secure configuration, modern classical encryption, monitoring and lockouts.
- **S3 Hybrid/software PQC:** strong protocols but software-held keys and endpoint risks.
- **S4 Segmented software PQC:** multiple controllers, rotation, attestation abstractions, monitoring and staged access.
- **S5 Fortress software-PQC biometric gate:** layered biometric/access/network controllers with software-only PQC roots; multiple target-specific quantum breach packages may be required.
- **S6 Certified on-chip PQC root:** direct fictional quantum cryptanalytic path is closed for protected operations; attackers must change strategy.

Security is multidimensional. A high biometric tier with poor firmware or a weak connected gate motor controller may remain vulnerable. A mechanical lock has no cryptographic attack surface.

# Quantum breach doctrine

DayQ's quantum breach is fictional. Ground its gameplay in physical cost, target specificity, uncertainty, and defender architecture.

It does **not** mean that real standardized PQC is known to be breakable. Present-day NIST standards are designed to resist future quantum attacks. DayQ's collapse and 2058 sovereign quantum systems are fiction.

## Direct target boundary

- `exposed_software` and `sealed_software` PQC key paths may be attacked through fictional endpoint-assisted quantum methods.
- `secure_element` paths require more intelligence, stages, energy, time, and may force attacks on surrounding firmware, authentication, or controllers.
- `certified_hardware_root` sets `quantum_direct_path: closed_by_hardware_root`.

The hardware-rooted system may still be defeated through another supported gameplay route, but the game must explicitly say the direct quantum cryptanalytic attempt is invalid rather than merely increasing a hidden health bar.

## Quantum attack agent progression

- **Q0 Observer:** recognizes cryptographic and trust architecture.
- **Q1 Fingerprinter:** extracts target profiles from authorized/recovered evidence.
- **Q2 Classical intrusion specialist:** maps endpoints, credentials, firmware, and connected controllers.
- **Q3 PQC implementation analyst:** finds software-only integration and operational weaknesses.

- **Q6 Sovereign adversarial cell:** coordinates multiple agents, Quantum ASIC stacks, target samples, counterfactuals, and staged operations; remains unable to directly break certified on-chip roots.

Training requires curricula, recovered research, target families, simulation, evaluator suites, held-out targets, human specialists, quantum compute, classical compute, power, cooling, secure tools, adversarial testing, and signed certification.

Fielded agents do not become quantum specialists merely through elapsed time or ordinary kills.

The agent's general skill persists. The target-specific package does not.

## Multi-stage fortress gate example

An S5 software-PQC biometric gate may expose several independently protected stages:

1. external network/service identity;
2. biometric template/decision service;
3. authorization policy controller;
4. actuator command channel;
5. alarm and monitoring path;
6. mechanical lock/bolt state.

A clan may need multiple breach packages, each targeting a different eligible stage. Success at one stage creates a bounded window or new access condition; it does not subtract abstract “encryption hit points.” Defenders may rotate keys, isolate stages, replace controllers, trigger alarms, require manual confirmation, or close the path between attacks.

If any protected stage is rooted in certified on-chip PQC hardware and is essential to the requested effect, the direct quantum path stops at that stage. The attackers must capture a credential, compromise a weaker connected controller, gain physical access, coerce an insider, attack power/mechanics, or destroy/breach the gate conventionally.
